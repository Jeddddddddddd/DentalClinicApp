Our Story

Over 15 Years of Caring for Smiles

BrightSmile Dental Clinic started as a small family dentist with a simple goal: make dental care gentle, affordable and stress-free. Today we are a full-service dental clinic trusted by families for checkups, teeth whitening, braces and dental implants.

  1. 2010Clinic opens with two dental chairs and a small, caring team.
  2. 2015Digital X-ray and orthodontics (braces and clear aligners) added.
  3. 2020Kids dentistry corner and online appointment booking launched.
  4. TodayA full-service clinic caring for families every day.
